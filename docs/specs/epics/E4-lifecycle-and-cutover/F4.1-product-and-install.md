## S4.1.1 — Product declaration and checks

**State:** New

**Acceptance:**

- Lifecycle `doctor` reports a missing `jq` and an unwritable share dir as
  errors (tests with a temp `RNF_HOME`).
- Install from a local archive into a temp `RNF_HOME` produces
  `$RNF_HOME/agentkit/versions/<v>/`, `current`, and `$RNF_HOME/bin/agentkit`;
  `agentkit --version` runs from the link.
- Uninstall leaves the share dir in place.
